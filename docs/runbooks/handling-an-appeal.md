# Handling an appeal

A person appeals a warning, limit, suspension or ban from their account or the link in the email. It lands in the **Inbox** as an appeal, with a 7-day target.

## Who decides

**Not whoever took the step.** The server refuses them while another admin who holds `appeals.decide` can be reached. If you're alone, you can decide it, and "same reviewer" shows on the decision and in the audit log for anyone who reads it later. Say so in your reason.

## Do

1. **Claim it**, then open it. You'll see what they said, the decision with its reason and clause, and everything else on the account.
2. Read the evidence on the account's own screens: the abuse item, the sessions, the report. Their attachments are their own uploads, listed by id.
3. Decide, with a reason the person will read. Write it plainly, with no one else's details.
   - **Keep the decision:** they're told at once. An appeal can be made only once per step.
   - **Reverse it:** another admin approves the lift, as with any lift. The appeal stays open until it runs. If the approval is rejected, it stays open for you to decide again.

## Things to know

- Reversing a suspension or ban lets the account back only if no other step still holds it.
- Reversing doesn't refund anything by itself. If money was affected, use the usual refund screens, with their own approvals.
- The strike goes when a step is lifted. Otherwise it stops counting after 180 days.
